For developers

Each series is one CSV with its full history, at a URL that does not change:

https://data.xfina.dev/v1/metadata.json lists every series with its path, columns, source, licence, row count, date range, sha256 and last update; read it to discover what is available and whether anything is new.

Downloads are compressed with Brotli or gzip when the client asks for it, which brings the larger series to about a quarter of their size (the BIS rates go from 254 KB to 65 KB). With curl:

curl --compressed -O https://data.xfina.dev/v1/fx/bis-usd-inr.csv

Over plain HTTP, ask with Accept-Encoding and the response says which one it used. Browsers and most HTTP libraries do this on their own:

GET /v1/fx/bis-usd-inr.csv HTTP/2
Host: data.xfina.dev
Accept-Encoding: br, gzip

HTTP/2 200
content-type: text/csv; charset=utf-8
content-encoding: br
access-control-allow-origin: *

Paths and columns under /v1/ are only ever added to, never renamed or removed.

Built by xfina-data. Each dataset's source and licence is on its page and in metadata.json.
